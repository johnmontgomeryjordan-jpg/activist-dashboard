What this is

A predictive screen that finds companies likely to attract an activist investor — before the activist starts building a position — so FGS can get in the door early. It ranks the S&P 1500 on the traits activists screen for, measured against each company's industry peers, using free public data.

How the rating works

Each company is scored on traits measured relative to its industry peers plus recent event accelerants. Those signals become a 0–92 activist-target profile index, weighted by how severe each one is, shown as a rating band: 75+ Severe 50–74 High 25–49 Elevated <25 Moderate It measures how strongly a company matches the profile activists target — it is not a probability that a campaign will happen, and is capped below 100 so it never implies certainty.

Where the data comes from (all free)

  • SEC XBRL fundamentals — revenue, margins, SG&A, returns, balance sheet vs peers.
  • SEC EDGAR filings — 8-K / 10-K / 10-Q events, Form 4 insider trades, 8-K 5.07 vote results, and a 13D / contested-proxy sweep for active situations.
  • Finnhub — market cap, price-to-book, P/E, 1-yr return, and the earnings calendar.
  • News API — activist / distress headlines, grouped on the Live feed.

How often it updates

  • Every 30 minutes — news, filings, market cap & valuation refresh and scores recompute.
  • Daily (4 PM ET) — full SEC fundamentals, governance, insider, votes, and the universe-wide activist sweep, then the email digest.
