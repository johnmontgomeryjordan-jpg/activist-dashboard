What this is

A predictive screen that finds companies likely to attract an activist investor — before the activist starts building a position — so FGS can get in the door early. It ranks the entire S&P 1500 on the structural traits activists screen for, measured against each company's industry peers, using free public data.

How the score works

Each company is scored on traits measured relative to its industry peers (grouped by SIC code), plus recent event "accelerants" that signal it's time to reach out. Flagged at/above the threshold (currently 4); the highest scorers become the shortlist.

Where the data comes from (all free)

  • SEC XBRL financials — each company's actual reported revenue, margins, SG&A, returns, and balance sheet, compared to industry peers. The core of the predictive screen.
  • SEC EDGAR filings — 8-K / 10-K / 10-Q events (CEO changes, impairments, guidance cuts) used as timing accelerants and shown in the live feed.
  • Financial news API — activist / distress headlines (headline + outlet + link only).
  • Alpha Vantage — market cap, valuation multiples, and a company overview, pulled for the shortlisted companies to power the detail view.

The company detail view

Click any flagged company to open a full profile: a plain-English overview, the exact signals it triggered, SEC-derived financials and Alpha Vantage market data, its recent SEC filings, and recent related news — everything needed to prep a pitch.

How often it updates

  • Every 30 minutes — news + EDGAR filings refresh and scores recompute.
  • Daily — full SEC fundamentals refresh, shortlist enrichment, and the 4:00 PM ET email digest.

Current limits & roadmap

  • Valuation coverage: on the free data tier, market-cap/valuation is pulled for the shortlist (not all 1,500). A paid market-data tier would extend valuation and multi-year total-return signals across the whole universe.
  • Classification is a strong first pass but can occasionally mis-tag a filing, so the shortlist is a lead-generation starting point for human review — not a final determination.
