What this is

A predictive screen that finds companies likely to attract an activist investor — before the activist starts building a position — so FGS can get in the door early. It ranks the entire S&P 1500 on the structural traits activists screen for, measured against each company's industry peers, using free public data.

How the score works

Each company is scored on traits measured relative to its industry peers (grouped by SIC code) from its latest quarterly SEC filing, plus recent event "accelerants" that signal it's time to reach out. Flagged at/above the threshold (currently 4); the highest scorers become the shortlist. Open any company to see the exact number, the underlying SEC math, and the peer cutoff behind every signal — each linked to its source filing. Delisted or acquired names are dropped automatically, and companies where an activist is already engaged are moved to a separate "active situations" list.

Where the data comes from (all free)

  • SEC XBRL financials — each company's actual reported revenue, margins, SG&A, returns, and balance sheet from its latest 10-Q, compared to industry peers. The core of the predictive screen, and the source for the line-item math.
  • SEC EDGAR filings — 8-K / 10-K / 10-Q events (CEO changes, impairments, guidance cuts) used as timing accelerants and shown in the live feed, grouped by type.
  • Financial news API — activist / distress headlines (headline + outlet + link only), grouped into categories you can open.
  • Alpha Vantage — market cap, valuation multiples, and a company overview, pulled only for the shortlisted companies. The only market-price input.

The company detail view

Click any flagged company to open a full profile: quick links out (Yahoo, Google, EDGAR, IR), a plain-English overview, and the proof behind each signal — the value, the exact SEC math, the fiscal period, the peer cutoff with sample size, and a link straight to the source filing. Followed by SEC-derived financials, recent filings, and recent related news. Star it to add to the shared watchlist.

How often it updates

  • Every 30 minutes — news + EDGAR filings refresh and scores recompute.
  • Daily — full SEC fundamentals refresh (latest quarter), shortlist enrichment, delisting checks, and the 4:00 PM ET email digest.

Current limits & roadmap

  • Valuation coverage: on the free data tier, market-cap/valuation is pulled for the shortlist (not all 1,500). A paid market-data tier would extend valuation and live price/movement across the whole universe.
  • One-time charges: a big one-time impairment in a quarter will depress the margin/return signals for that period — usually a real vulnerability, but worth a glance at the filing.
  • Classification is a strong first pass but can occasionally mis-tag a filing, so the shortlist is a lead-generation starting point for human review — not a final determination.
